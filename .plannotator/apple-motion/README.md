# Apple motion research and proof

This archive records the original approved motion study. The brief later expanded into the [Apple design and animation system](../apple-design-and-animation/README.md). The original plan and preview notes retain their historical names and scope.

The [motion lab](lab.html) demonstrates bounded slider values with shell stretching, velocity-projected snapping, and smooth-union separation. [Numerical tests](motion.test.mjs), [browser observations](browser-evidence.json), desktop and narrow captures, and the [replay recording](videos/proposed-motion-mechanisms.mp4) record the checks performed during that stage. The replay uses simulated input. It does not establish native Apple timing or visual fidelity.

[Research](research/README.md) contains original synthesis and retrieval manifests. Downloaded Apple videos, frame extractions, full source pages, transcripts, papers, third-party source files, and recorder caches remain local under this directory's ignore policy. Their original URLs and retrieval hashes remain in the selected manifests.

The current skill and runnable examples are maintained in [maynkudu/apple-design-and-animation-system](https://github.com/maynkudu/apple-design-and-animation-system). This archive is review evidence rather than a second installation source.
