### [Manage how Google's crawlers and fetchers interact with your website](http://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/overview-google-crawlers)

Learning how Google's user agents interact with your website can help get your most important pages appearing in more places in the Google ecosystem, without overloading your servers. Whether you're a site owner or developer, this documentation gives you better control over how your site is crawled. [Learn more](http://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/overview-google-crawlers)

### [Verify requests from Google crawlers and fetchers](http://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/verify-google-requests)

You can verify if a web crawler or fetcher accessing your server really is from Google, such as Googlebot. [Get started](http://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/verify-google-requests)

### [Reduce the Google crawl rate](http://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/reduce-crawl-rate)

Google's crawlers automatically adjust to your website and server to efficiently find and showcase your best and freshest content. To help you manage urgent situations related to your server and crawling, you have the option to proactively reduce the crawl rate, if needed. [Get started](http://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/reduce-crawl-rate)

### [Manage crawling with your robots.txt file](http://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/robots-txt/create-robots-txt)

You can use a robots.txt file to allow or disallow crawling of individual pages, or your whole website. [Get started](http://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/robots-txt/create-robots-txt)

## How crawling preferences affect where your site appears on Google

Google's crawling infrastructure is shared across a variety of Google products. This means that following best practices helps your web content be discovered more efficiently and featured on Google. [![](https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/productlogos/google_search_round/v8/48px.svg)](http://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/googlebot)

### [Google Search](http://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/googlebot)

Google Search uses Googlebot to crawl your site to find relevant content for users. [![](https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/productlogos/google_adsense/v5/192px.svg)](http://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/google-common-crawlers#google-extended)

### [Gemini](http://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/google-common-crawlers#google-extended)

Google-Extended is used by Gemini apps and Vertex AI API for Gemini. [Learn more about Google-Extended](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/google-common-crawlers#google-extended). [![](https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/productlogos/google_adsense/v5/192px.svg)](http://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/google-common-crawlers#google-storebot)

### [Google Shopping](http://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/google-common-crawlers#google-storebot)

Crawling preferences addressed to the Storebot-Google user agent affect all surfaces of Google Shopping. [![](https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/productlogos/google_adsense/v5/192px.svg)](http://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/google-special-case-crawlers#adsbot)

### [Google Ads](http://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/google-special-case-crawlers#adsbot)

Crawling preferences addressed to the AdsBot-Google user agent affect Google Ads' ability to check web page ad quality. [![](https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/productlogos/google_adsense/v5/192px.svg)](http://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/google-common-crawlers#googlebot-news)

### [Google News](http://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/google-common-crawlers#googlebot-news)

Crawling preferences addressed to the Googlebot-News user agent affect the Google News product, including news.google.com and the Google News app. [![](http://developers.google.com/static/crawling/images/gemini-notebook__full-color.png)](http://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/google-user-triggered-fetchers#gemini-notebook)

### [Gemini Notebook](http://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/google-user-triggered-fetchers#gemini-notebook)

The Google-GeminiNotebook fetcher requests individual URLs that Gemini Notebook users have provided as sources for their projects.
