## How to get your website on Google Search

Google Search Central, formerly Google Webmasters, is here to help the right people view your content with resources to make your website discoverable to Google Search. New to SEO? Start by reading this introductory guide to SEO.
[Get started](http://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide)

## Specific things you can do to improve the SEO of your website

Select your profile and get some recommendations of how you can improve the SEO of your website below. ![](http://developers.google.com/static/search/images/home-Card1-business_480.png)

### Business owner or marketer

I want to learn the basics of optimizing site for search engines, and know what features are available in Google Search. ### Developer

I create the markup and code for my site. I'm interested in monitoring and resolving common issues with how my site shows up on Google Search. Creates the infrastructure for hosting the content ![](http://developers.google.com/static/search/images/home-Card3-seo_480.png)

### SEO professional

I already know the basics of how Google Search works. I'm focused on monitoring website traffic, optimizing ranking, and making informed decisions about the appearance of my site in Search results. Optimizes ranking in Google Search My website... Has multiple languages Uses JavaScript My business also... Has a storefront Is about an entity (such as a large brand or famous person)

## Why Google Search?

Browse SEO case studies and learn how other sites have reached more people by improving their site's presence on Google Search. Case study

### [Saramin increased organic Search traffic 2x](http://developers.google.com/search/case-studies/saramin-case-study)

In addition to seeing a traffic increase, Saramin saw the quality of their traffic improve. They saw a 93% increase in the number of new sign ups and a 9% increase on the conversion. [Read the case study](http://developers.google.com/search/case-studies/saramin-case-study) Case study

### [MX Player boosts organic traffic 3x](http://developers.google.com/search/case-studies/mx-case-study)

After adding Video structured data and following best practices, MX Player saw over 3x growth in traffic from Google and a 100% increase of video page views per user session on their platform from organic Search traffic. [Read the case study](http://developers.google.com/search/case-studies/mx-case-study) Case study

### [ZipRecruiter grows conversion rate 4.5x](http://developers.google.com/search/case-studies/ziprecruiter-case-study)

After the launch of `JobPosting` structured data, Google organic traffic to ZipRecruiter job pages converted at a rate three times higher than organic traffic from other search engines. [Read the case study](http://developers.google.com/search/case-studies/ziprecruiter-case-study) [![](http://developers.google.com/static/search/images/search-central-new-updates.png)](http://developers.google.com/search/news)
